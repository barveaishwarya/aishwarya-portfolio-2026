03 / HOW I THINK

Before pixels,
people.

Good design starts with listening.
Then comes the making, testing, and refining.

01Understand ↗

Ask questions. Listen closely. Explore the problem through user research and build an understanding of the people behind it.

02Explore ↗

Turn insights into user flows, sketches, and wireframes. Explore possibilities before committing to a visual direction.

03Make it tangible ↗

Shape the visual language, build prototypes, and connect the small details into an experience that feels coherent.

04Test & refine ↗

Observe how people use the experience. Learn where it feels effortless, where it falls short, and what to improve next.
